# Page Content Plan — Phase 1 Output
## خطة محتوى الصفحات

For EVERY must-have page listed in `01-profile.md`, define its required
sections, the real content for each, AND why that section belongs on this
page. This is what Phase 2 reads to build each page's first draft. No
design/style decisions here (palette, fonts, layout treatment) — content
and structure only.

## Go beyond the generic skeleton

Don't default to a thin Header/Hero/Overview/CTA/Footer template for every
page. Different industries and page purposes call for different sections —
think about what THIS visitor, on THIS page, for THIS industry actually
needs to see before they'd trust the business or act. Examples of sections
beyond the basics, use whichever genuinely fit:

- **Trust Stats Band** — numbers build credibility fast (years in
  business, fleet/team size, clients served, coverage area). Use
  qualitative labels instead of invented numbers if real figures aren't
  confirmed yet.
- **How It Works / Process** — reduces "what happens after I submit this
  form" hesitation, especially for service/B2B businesses.
- **Industries/Segments We Serve** — when the business serves visibly
  different buyer types (e.g. pharma vs. restaurants), naming them
  explicitly builds relevance faster than generic copy.
- **Comparison / Selector Table** — when a business offers multiple
  similar options (services, tiers, products) and the visitor needs to
  self-identify which one applies to them.
- **FAQ** — answers objections before they become reasons to leave;
  scope it to what's actually relevant on that specific page, not generic
  filler questions.
- **Certifications / Compliance / Technology** — a fast trust shortcut
  for buyers who won't read long trust copy, especially relevant for
  regulated or technical industries.
- **Timeline / History** — visualizes longevity better than a sentence
  claiming "long experience," if the business has real milestones.
- **Coverage/Service Area** — for any location-dependent business,
  answer "do they serve me" before the visitor has to ask.

Every section still needs a real reason tied to this specific client's
industry and goal — don't add sections just to pad length. If a section
doesn't have a clear "why," it doesn't belong.

## Notes

- If the client's profile doesn't have enough real content for a section
  (e.g. no testimonials, no exact fleet count, no certifications given),
  mark it as "Needs client input" rather than inventing fake numbers or
  claims. The section can still be structured and included — just flagged
  — rather than omitted entirely, if it's genuinely valuable once filled in.
- Optional sections (e.g. Team/Leadership, Office Location) should be
  explicitly marked optional so Phase 2 doesn't have to guess whether to
  build them.
- This plan should be specific enough that Phase 2 doesn't have to guess
  what belongs on each page or why — it only has to decide how it looks.
