# Page Content Plan — Al-Ziyad Company
## خطة محتوى الصفحات

Every section below includes WHY it exists — a B2B logistics buyer
evaluates a transport company differently than a typical retail visitor:
they want proof of capability (fleet, temperature control, coverage),
proof of reliability (process, compliance, track record), and an easy way
to get a quote once convinced. The structure reflects that.

---

## Page: Home

**Purpose:** Convert a first-time visitor's trust into a quote request,
fast — a logistics buyer decides "credible or not" within seconds.

**Required sections, in order:**

1. **Header/Nav**
   - Content: Logo, nav (Home, Services, Our Fleet, About Us, Contact),
     phone number visible in nav bar.
   - Why: Phone-in-nav matters specifically for B2B logistics — many
     buyers call before they'd ever fill a form.

2. **Hero**
   - Content: Headline from "رواد في خدمات الشحن والنقل المبرد," subline
     covering all 3 services in one sentence, primary CTA ("Request a
     Quote"), background: real daytime fleet photo.
   - Why: Sets category + credibility immediately — visitor should know
     "temperature-controlled transport company" within 2 seconds.

3. **Trust Stats Band**
   - Content: 3-4 numbers in a row — e.g. fleet size, years of experience,
     cities/regions covered, markets served (Egypt + Saudi Arabia). Marked
     **Needs client input** for exact figures (fleet count, years) — use
     placeholders like "10+ Trucks" only if the client confirms a real
     number, otherwise use qualitative labels ("Modern Fleet," "Egypt &
     Saudi Arabia Coverage") instead of inventing numbers.
   - Why: Numbers are the fastest trust signal in logistics — buyers
     compare fleet size and coverage before anything else. A qualitative
     version still works if hard numbers aren't available yet.

4. **Services Overview**
   - Content: 3 cards — Frozen (-18°C), Chilled (0-8°C), Dry — one-line
     description each, icon per service, link to that section on Services.
   - Why: Visitor self-sorts into their actual need immediately.

5. **How It Works**
   - Content: 4-step process — e.g. Request a Quote → Confirm Pickup →
     Temperature-Controlled Transit → On-Time Delivery. Content not in
     source docs — **Needs client input** to confirm actual process steps,
     but the section itself is standard and valuable for this industry.
   - Why: Logistics buyers want to know what happens after they submit a
     form — reduces the "will someone actually respond" hesitation that
     kills B2B conversions.

6. **Industries We Serve**
   - Content: Grid of sectors pulled from source docs — Food & Beverage,
     Pharmaceuticals, Dairy, Retail & Restaurants, Distributors/Wholesalers.
   - Why: A pharma buyer and a restaurant supplier have different
     concerns; naming their industry explicitly builds "this company
     understands businesses like mine" faster than generic copy.

7. **Why Al-Ziyad (Differentiators)**
   - Content: 4 points from source copy — modern fleet & tech, strict
     food-safety compliance, on-time delivery commitment, trained team +
     customized solutions.
   - Why: Directly answers "why you and not a competitor."

8. **Our Fleet (teaser)**
   - Content: 2-3 real daytime fleet photos, one-line intro, link to Our
     Fleet page.
   - Why: Real fleet photos are a genuine asset here — most competitors
     use stock photography, so showing actual trucks is a differentiator.

9. **Coverage Area**
   - Content: Egypt (primary) + Saudi Arabia (expansion), ideally as a
     simple labeled map or region list. Exact cities/regions **Needs
     client input**.
   - Why: "Do they even serve my city/route" is a disqualifying question
     for logistics buyers — answering it up front avoids wasted inquiries.

10. **FAQ**
    - Content: 3-4 questions a logistics buyer actually asks — e.g. "How
      is temperature monitored during transit?", "What's the minimum
      shipment size?", "Do you serve Saudi Arabia directly?", "How fast
      can you confirm a quote?" Answers drawn from source copy where
      possible; mark speculative ones **Needs client input**.
    - Why: Answers objections before they become reasons not to submit
      the form — cheaper than a phone call to resolve the same doubt.

11. **CTA Band**
    - Content: "تواصل معنا الآن لطلب عرض سعر" + phone/WhatsApp buttons +
      link to Contact.
    - Why: Every major section above should be able to end here — this
      is the conversion point the whole page builds toward.

12. **Footer**
    - Content: Logo, contact numbers, fax, nav links repeated, markets
      served note.
    - Why: Standard wayfinding + a last-chance contact surface.

**RTL notes:** Nav mirrors right, CTA icons mirrored, phone numbers stay
LTR-formatted inside RTL flow, stats band reads right-to-left in order.

## Page: Services

**Purpose:** Give a logistics buyer enough specific, technical confidence
in each service that they trust Al-Ziyad with their actual cargo type.

**Required sections, in order:**

1. **Header/Nav** — same as Home.

2. **Page Intro**
   - Content: One line framing all 3 services as one integrated fleet
     capability, not three separate businesses.
   - Why: Reinforces "one reliable partner for all your temperature
     needs" rather than reading as a fragmented offering.

3. **Service Selector / Comparison Table**
   - Content: A compact table — Service | Temp Range | Best For — showing
     Frozen/Chilled/Dry side by side so a buyer can self-identify their
     need in one glance before reading details.
   - Why: B2B buyers often arrive not knowing exact terminology ("do I
     need frozen or chilled for this?") — a comparison table resolves
     that faster than three separate walls of text.

4. **Frozen Transport (النقل المجمد)**
   - Content: -18°C or lower. For meat, poultry, fish, ice cream. Bullets:
     precise temperature monitoring, strict food-safety compliance.
   - Why: Detailed enough that a food-safety-conscious buyer (e.g. meat
     distributor) can verify Al-Ziyad meets their requirement.

5. **Chilled Transport (النقل المبرد)**
   - Content: 0-8°C. For dairy, juices, fresh fruit/vegetables. Bullets:
     advanced refrigeration tech, suits restaurants/retail/factories.

6. **Dry Transport (النقل الجاف)**
   - Content: No refrigeration. For dry goods, detergents, consumables.
     Bullets: clean/safe trucks, suits distributors/wholesalers, fast
     intra-city and cross-region delivery.

7. **Equipment & Technology**
   - Content: Brief note on the fleet/trailer tech used (Chereau
     refrigerated trailers, confirmed from fleet photos) with a link to
     the Our Fleet page for full detail.
   - Why: Avoids duplicating the full fleet story here, while still
     giving a technical buyer a reason to click through and verify
     capability.

8. **FAQ (Services-specific)**
   - Content: e.g. "Can one shipment mix chilled and frozen goods?", "Is
     temperature logged and shared with clients?" — mark **Needs client
     input** where source docs don't confirm.
   - Why: Same rationale as Home's FAQ, scoped to service-level technical
     objections rather than general company trust.

9. **CTA Band**
   - Content: "Not sure which service fits your cargo? Contact us."
   - Why: Some visitors will still be unsure after reading all three —
     give them a low-effort way to ask rather than bouncing.

10. **Footer** — same as Home.

**RTL notes:** Comparison table columns/reading order flip for RTL; each
service block mirrors as one unit (icon + text together, not just text).

## Page: Our Fleet

**Purpose:** Turn the real fleet photos into concrete proof of scale,
maintenance standards, and professionalism.

**Required sections, in order:**

1. **Header/Nav** — same as Home.

2. **Page Intro**
   - Content: Modern fleet, Mercedes Actros tractor units, Chereau
     refrigerated trailers, real operational scale.
   - Why: Frames the gallery that follows — visitor should know what
     they're looking at before scrolling through photos.

3. **Fleet Stats Band**
   - Content: Fleet size (count), truck models represented (Actros
     1843/1844/1846 — confirmed from photos), trailer brand (Chereau —
     confirmed). Exact count **Needs client input**.
   - Why: Same trust-through-numbers logic as Home, but fleet-specific —
     this page's whole job is proving capacity.

4. **Fleet Gallery**
   - Content: Real daytime fleet photos only (clear, well-lit shots with
     visible branding — exclude poorly lit night shots). Each photo can
     carry a short caption (model, trailer type).
   - Why: This is the actual differentiating asset — most competitor
     sites use generic stock trucks; real photography is proof, not
     decoration.

5. **Temperature Monitoring & Technology**
   - Content: How temperature is controlled/monitored in transit (GPS
     tracking, temperature logging, alerts). **Needs client input** —
     source docs mention "precise monitoring" but not the specific
     technology/method used.
   - Why: For pharma/food buyers specifically, "how do you prove the cold
     chain wasn't broken" is often the single biggest question — this
     section exists to answer it once it's confirmed.

6. **Maintenance & Safety Standards**
   - Content: **Needs client input** — any certifications (ISO, HACCP),
     maintenance schedule, driver training standards. Source docs
     mention "trained team" generally but no specific certifications.
   - Why: Certifications are a fast trust shortcut for buyers who don't
     want to read detailed copy — worth adding once available, even if
     it launches without this section initially.

7. **CTA Band**
   - Content: "Want to see if we cover your route? Contact us."

8. **Footer** — same as Home.

**RTL notes:** Gallery grid reads right-to-left in scan order; stats band
mirrors like Home's.

## Page: About Us

**Purpose:** Build the "who is behind this company" trust layer — this
page has the richest existing source copy of any page.

**Required sections, in order:**

1. **Header/Nav** — same as Home.

2. **من نحن (Who We Are)**
   - Content: Directly from source "من نحن" doc — leading refrigerated
     transport company in Egypt, modern fleet, serves food/pharma/temp-
     sensitive sectors, strict schedule adherence.

3. **Company Timeline / History**
   - Content: **Needs client input** — founding year, key milestones
     (fleet expansion, market entry into Saudi Arabia, etc.). Structure
     the section to hold a timeline even before exact dates are
     confirmed — it's a valuable format for a company with "long-standing
     experience" once the details exist.
   - Why: A timeline visualizes longevity better than a sentence saying
     "long experience" — concrete milestones read as more credible than
     the same claim in prose.

4. **رؤيتنا (Vision)**
   - Content: From source — aims to be the first choice in Egypt and the
     region for refrigerated transport, built on quality/reliability,
     continuous fleet expansion.

5. **رسالتنا (Mission)**
   - Content: From source — integrated shipping solutions, guarantees:
     product safety in transit, precise delivery timing, wide coverage
     within Egypt and Saudi Arabia.

6. **Core Values**
   - Content: Derived from repeated themes across source docs — Safety,
     Punctuality, Quality, Customer-Centricity. Framed as a short values
     grid rather than prose repetition of Vision/Mission.
   - Why: Vision/Mission are aspirational; a values grid restates the
     same substance in a scannable format for visitors who won't read
     two full paragraphs.

7. **Team / Leadership**
   - Content: **Needs client input**, optional — include only if the
     client wants to put names/faces to the company; skip if not.
   - Why: Builds trust further for B2B buyers who prefer knowing who
     they're dealing with, but not essential — flagged as optional
     rather than required.

8. **Certifications & Compliance**
   - Content: **Needs client input** — same as Our Fleet's Maintenance
     section; if certifications exist, they belong on both pages (Fleet
     for operational proof, About for company-level credibility).

9. **CTA Band**
   - Content: "جرب الفرق مع شركة تجمع بين الخبرة والجودة."

10. **Footer** — same as Home.

**RTL notes:** Timeline should flow right-to-left (earliest milestone on
the right if using a horizontal timeline layout).

## Page: Contact

**Purpose:** The actual lead-generation mechanism — lowest possible
friction between "convinced" and "submitted a quote request."

**Required sections, in order:**

1. **Header/Nav** — same as Home.

2. **Page Intro**
   - Content: "تواصل معنا الآن لطلب عرض سعر" + one line reassuring fast
     response time. Response time itself **Needs client input** (e.g.
     "within 1 business hour") — don't invent a number.

3. **Direct Contact**
   - Content: Phone (01067892154), secondary phone (01060740074), Fax
     (0132761591), WhatsApp button linked to primary number. Email
     **Needs client input** — none confirmed in source material.

4. **Quote Request Form**
   - Content: Name, Phone, Service Needed (dropdown: Frozen / Chilled /
     Dry / Not sure), Message (optional). Single short form, one submit
     button — no multi-step flow, per earlier decision.

5. **Service Area Reference**
   - Content: Short repeat of coverage (Egypt + Saudi Arabia) with a
     prompt like "Don't see your city? Ask us — we're expanding."
   - Why: Keeps the coverage question answered at the exact moment
     someone is about to submit a form, reducing last-second drop-off
     from buyers unsure if they're even in range.

6. **Office/Depot Location** (if applicable)
   - Content: **Needs client input** — physical address(es) if the
     client wants a location shown/mapped; skip if not applicable to a
     transport company without a public office front.

7. **Footer** — same as Home.

**RTL notes:** Form fields right-aligned, labels positioned per RTL
convention, WhatsApp icon mirrored to the right of its button.

## Notes

- Sections marked **Needs client input** don't block starting Phase 2 —
  they can launch with a qualitative placeholder (e.g. "Modern Fleet"
  instead of a fake number) and be filled in once real figures are
  confirmed, without restructuring the page.
- Every section not marked "Needs client input" is built from the
  client's own uploaded documents and photos — not invented copy.
- Optional sections (Team/Leadership) are explicitly flagged as optional
  so Phase 2 doesn't have to guess whether to include them.
